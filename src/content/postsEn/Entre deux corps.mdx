## Project starting point

Group project in the Ouro valley, in Porto. The valley is there, visible from the heights, but cut off from the neighbourhood by the relief and the cars.
My intervention sits on a cliff side, from a raised neighbourhood. The project takes the form of an intergenerational games library that fits into a terracing system designed to make the slope passable.
The challenge was not to build an object, but to create an entry point towards the park: a threshold between the upper city and the valley.

## Crossing

Archicad, Midjourney, Photoshop used.

Start from an observation in the Ouro neighbourhood: the valley is crossed, never inhabited.
Take the margins today occupied by cars as raw material.
Turn them into "gates": open thresholds that guide planted routes and slow down movement.
Compose atmospheres rather than segments: pauses, encounters, suspended gazes.
The games library slots into this system: two volumes on the cliff, separated by a central passage.
This passage is a real threshold: shelter, halt, viewpoint, then departure towards the Douro.
Choreographing the revelation: the bridge, the river, the valley appear like a spectacle.
No isolated object. Just a crossing.

## Critical review

The terracing makes the slope accessible, but it erases it. We no longer follow the topography, we correct it. The descent becomes a controlled path where one could have experienced something more physical.
The games library completes the group project with points of attraction that invite people towards the valley, but it is not enough to activate it. It waits for a user who will decide to commit to the descent. This dependence weakens the whole.
The project lacks a strong point of attraction at the top, able to link everything together. One could have taken the slope as a starting point, made it a structuring constraint instead of fighting it.

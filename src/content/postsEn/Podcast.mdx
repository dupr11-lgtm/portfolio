## Starting point

A studio during my Erasmus in Stuttgart: making a podcast, in pairs, about a workshop and a site, Ebelsbach Castle.
My idea was to shift architectural practice towards speech. No plans, no models, just voice to get the intentions across.
The episode weaves together two stories: the students’ projects on one side, and on the other the approach of the association provinz:potenzial. Here, speech does not illustrate the project. It is the project.
Available on all the usual platforms: ‘Double Vision: Architecture Abroad’

## Walk-through

Shift architecture into another medium.
In Stuttgart, record an episode documenting a workshop: final crits, intentions, methods, differences.
Around Ebelsbach, two stories intertwine: the students’ projects and the approach of the collective provinz:potenzial.
Voice as a design tool: to convey, to clarify, to share.
Drawing is still there, but it is no longer alone.
We are not building a space; we are building access to one.

## Critical reflection

Changing medium forces you to rephrase. What you cannot draw, you have to say, and that makes things clearer.
The podcast does justice to the students’ intentions, their ways of working and the differences between their approaches. That connection comes through clearly.
But without images, you depend entirely on what is said to picture the spaces. The podcast speaks mostly to people already familiar with this kind of language. That is less a flaw than a limit.
What I take from it: speech is a design tool in its own right. Drawing still matters, but it is no longer alone.

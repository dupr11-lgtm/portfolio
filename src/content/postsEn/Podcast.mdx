## Project starting point

Workshop during Erasmus in Stuttgart: creating a podcast, as a duo, around a workshop and a site: Ebelsbach castle.
My idea was to move architectural practice towards speech. No plan, no model, just the voice to convey intentions.
The episode weaves two narratives together: on one side, the students' projects; on the other, the approach of the association "provinz:potenzial". Here, speech does not illustrate the project: it is the project.
Available on all platforms: "Double Vision : Architecture Abroad"

## Crossing

Moving architecture towards another medium.
In Stuttgart, recording an episode that documents a workshop: final presentations, intentions, methods, gaps.
Around Ebelsbach, two stories intertwine: the students' projects and the approach of the collective provinz:potenzial.
The voice as a project tool: conveying, clarifying, sharing.
Drawing still exists, but it is no longer alone.
We do not build a space, we build access to space.

## Critical review

Changing medium forces you to reformulate. What you do not draw, you must say, and that clarifies.
The podcast conveys the students' intentions well, their way of working, the gap between approaches. This link is well established.
But without images, you depend entirely on discourse to imagine the spaces. The podcast mainly speaks to those who already know this register. It is not a flaw, but a limit.
What I take away: speech is a project tool in its own right. Drawing remains important, but it is no longer alone.

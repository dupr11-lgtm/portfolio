## Project starting point

In a school, a square.
Short exercise, done in three hours.
Making architecture an instrument.
Combining use, structure and sound.
Letting the wind, the rain and the bodies activate the space.

## Crossing

You enter the square.
A stretched canvas.
It gives shade.
The wind passes.
It vibrates.
A sound appears.
Further on, tubes.
They filter the gaze.
The wind blows.
The sound comes out, hesitant.
In the centre, a table.
You sit.
You rest.
You work.
Then you strike.
The wood resonates.
The rain falls.
The drops arrive.
They strike.
Repeat.
Further on, a mass.
A gong.
You touch it.
It answers.
For a long time.
Sounds do not add up.
They cross.
Answer each other.
Get lost.
No one directs.
You use.
Then you play.
Without really deciding.
The square becomes an active space.
Music appears.
Unstable.
Always different.
Never finished.

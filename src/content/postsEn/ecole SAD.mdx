## Starting point

In a school, a square.
A short exercise, done in three hours.
Make architecture an instrument.
Bring together use, structure and sound.
Let the wind, the rain and people’s bodies set the space in motion.

## Walk-through

You enter the square.
A stretched canvas.
It gives shade.
The wind goes by.
It vibrates.
A sound appears.
Further on, tubes.
They filter the view.
The wind blows.
The sound comes out, hesitant.
In the middle, a table.
You sit down.
You put things down.
You work.
Then you knock on it.
The wood resonates.
Rain falls.
The drops arrive.
They strike.
Again and again.
Further on, a mass.
A gong.
You touch it.
It answers.
For a long time.
The sounds don’t add up.
They cross.
Answer each other.
Get lost.
Nobody is in charge.
You use it.
Then you play.
Without really deciding to.
The square becomes an active space.
Music appears.
Unstable.
Always different.
Never finished.

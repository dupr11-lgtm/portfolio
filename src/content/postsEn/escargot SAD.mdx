## Starting point

A course exercise: design a fold-out leaflet for a project of your choice.
I chose to put in something slightly absurd, a house for a snail, because I was more interested in the format than the subject. A leaflet is an object you handle, one that reveals itself in stages.
The challenge was to get the physical object, the text and the folding to share one format without any of them overwhelming the others.

## Walk-through

Choose a snail.
A simple object, a very small house.
The leaflet becomes its jewel case.
In the centre, an empty space waiting. The shell moves in.
The front opens out like a poster: the snail on display.
Then the fold appears.
The leaflet closes, divides and turns: four panels, three stages of reading and a case of its own.
You handle it, open it, discover it piece by piece.
The project does not show everything at once. It invites you along the way.

## Critical reflection

The idea works: the object in the centre, the text around it, the folding as a reveal. It is clear, and it meets the brief.
But the leaflet does its job and goes no further. It holds the object rather than building a world around it. There was room to explore, the brief allowed it, and I did not take it.

## Project starting point

Course exercise: designing a folded leaflet for a free project.
I chose to put something a little absurd in it, a house for a snail, because the format interested me more than the subject. A leaflet is an object you handle, that reveals itself in stages.
The challenge was to make the physical object, the text and the folding coexist in a single medium, without one crushing the others.

## Crossing

Choosing a snail.
A simple object, a tiny house.
The leaflet becomes its setting.
In the centre, an empty space waiting. The shell takes its place.
The front opens like a poster: the snail on display.
Then the fold appears.
The leaflet closes, divides, turns: four flaps, three reading moments and an individual setting.
You handle, you open, you discover in fragments.
The project does not show everything at once; it offers a journey.

## Critical review

The idea works: the object in the centre, the text around it, the folding as revelation. It is clear and coherent with the brief.
But the leaflet fulfils its role without going beyond it. It contains the object instead of creating a universe around it. The room for exploration was there, the constraint allowed it, and I did not seize it.

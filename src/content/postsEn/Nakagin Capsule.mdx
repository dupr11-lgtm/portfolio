## Project starting point

Studio on individual practices. Starting point: the Nakagin Capsule Tower, modular architecture based on the repetition of identical capsules.
Each capsule, identical in appearance, becomes a surface to personalise.
I chose to turn a capsule into an intimate place: a photographic development laboratory. It is a closed, controlled space, entirely devoted to light.
My intervention works inside the cell: I modulate the light, create an atmosphere and play with temporality, that of waiting for the print.
The goal: to turn this standard cell into a singular, almost secret experience.

### Crossing

Start from a neutral form: an empty capsule, identical for everyone.
The Nakagin Tower: module, repetition, standardisation.
Inside, I look for difference.
Making the capsule a photo laboratory: a closed, almost secret place.
Working with light as material: it enters, changes, becomes an image.
Composing a slow atmosphere: waiting for the print, chemical smell, silence, concentration.
The space does not only host a use: it acts on perception.
Assembled, the capsules form a single structure, and yet each experience is unique.

## Critical review

The capsule imposes a tiny, repetitive volume. Here, this constraint becomes a resource.
By introducing photographic development, I move from an arrangement to a device: light is no longer simple lighting, it produces the image. It is a sharp break with the technical projects.
But the project closes in on itself. It is a closed, coherent world, with little dialogue with the outside, with the other capsules or with the tower. Its strength is also its withdrawal.

## Context

A writing exercise on the theme of intoxication.
A piece of work that took a year, somewhere between imagination and writing.
The course called for a long form, conceived as a book. Build a story out of a state of being.
Establish a character, a place, a setting.
Then let the writing develop an experience.

## Research notebook

The starting point: intoxication.
Not as excess. As distortion.
I am looking for a state in which perception slips.
I come across rapture of the deep.
From 30 metres down, nitrogen starts to take effect.
Movements slow.
Judgement clouds.
Reality becomes unstable.
Euphoria.
Confusion.
Tunnel vision.
Losing track of time.
Nothing is stable.
And above all, nothing is predictable.
Even the mechanism remains unclear.
Gas, membranes, proteins.
Nobody really knows.
That uncertainty becomes my material.
From there, I start building.
A character: Paul.
A place: Dahab, the Blue Hole.
A goal: to go down.
Everything is prepared.
Controlled.
And yet anything might give way.
The descent becomes something else.
A body going awry.
A space that acts.

## Context

Writing exercise around the theme of dizziness.
A year-long project, between imagination and writing.
The course framework imposes a long form, thought of as a book. Building a narrative from a state.
Setting up a character, a place, a context.
Then letting the writing develop an experience.

## Research notebook

The starting point: dizziness.
Not as excess. As distortion.
I look for a state where perception slips.
I come across the dizziness of the depths.
From 30 metres, nitrogen takes effect.
Movements slow down.
Judgement blurs.
Reality becomes unstable.
Euphoria.
Confusion.
Tunnel vision.
Loss of time.
Nothing is stable.
And above all, nothing is predictable.
Even the mechanism stays vague.
Gas, membranes, proteins.
We do not really know.
This vagueness becomes material.
From there, I build.
A character: Paul.
A place: Dahab, the Blue Hole.
A goal: to descend.
Everything is prepared.
Controlled.
And yet, everything can give way.
The descent becomes something else.
A body that malfunctions.
A space that acts.
